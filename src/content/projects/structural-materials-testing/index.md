- Generated S-N curves for 7075-T6 aluminum and 4140 steel from rotating-beam fatigue tests at 6,000 rpm (26 to 80 ksi). The steel showed an endurance limit that the aluminum lacked.
- Measured buckling stress falling from 35.5 to 11.4 ksi in 6061-T6 aluminum tubes as length rose from 4.5 to 10 in, and compared the results to Euler theory.
- Measured 67.5 ksi yield and 77.5 ksi ultimate strength in 7050-T7 aluminum from ASTM E8 tensile tests on an Instron 4505, within about 2% of published yield.
