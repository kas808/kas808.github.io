I earned my Rhinoceros Level 1 and Level 2 certifications through Robert McNeel & Associates, the company that makes Rhino.

I passed the Rhinoceros Level 1 Certification on May 22, 2020, after completing Parts A, B and C of the test on May 13, May 18 and May 22, 2020.
