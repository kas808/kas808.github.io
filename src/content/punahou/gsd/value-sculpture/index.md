# What is a Value Sculpture?

![Value Sculpture](./images/02.png)

To complete this project we had to use the "Design Thinking Process" (shown on left). We are going through each step to create a sculpture using six materials and six values. We also needed to use three different machines(water jet, CNC Router, laser cutter, or 3D printer). These items need to be attached using four methods of attachment. We sketched our ideas in our notebooks trying to get our best ideas out there. Then we brought these ideas into RHINO and tried many different variations to prototype. We then put all these ideas together to showcase our values and how we made them.

# Interconnected

Final Idea: My idea is to have a camera lens in the middle with a tree on top, placed on roots that branch out into three pedestals holding more values. I went through many different designs because I wanted each piece to feel more connected and this achieved it the best. # Done!

I am really happy with how my final product came out! I think it is a good representation of my values. I also am glad I chose this idea over the other ideas I had. This idea feels the most connected and makes the most amount of sense to me. I was able to use three different machines including the water jet(roots), laser cutter(tree), and the 3D printer(pedestals.) I also was able to use all four ways of connection including tape, glue, friction fit, and even velcro(mug to roots.) The overall look feels substantial but open and free which is what I was going for. I was afraid the bottom part wouldn't feel strong enough but I think it came out just the right amount strength with the three pillars. The tree with the paper leaves makes it feel more colorful as well as I think to capture the image I wanted as well of sort of a scrap-bookish look. It also allowed the addition of rocks which make the top part feel more substantial as well. The roots on the bottom also feel stronger because of the use of metal which also helps to symbolize the strong connection I have with my family. The camera lens in the middle stands out and I think gives the right effect. I think it can be considered sort of the centerpiece including the tree but since I enjoy photography I am glad it is a big part of the total whole. I am very happy with the end product and I think that this was a good project to help me to explore the variety of tools and items that we have in the shop getting me more experience with everything in there! I also think that this project can relate to UNSD Goal #12 Responsible Consumption and Production. It can relate to this because I tried to save as much material as I could. I think an example of this would be my root base because I test cut it in wood on the laser cutter before using metal on the water jet. This was beneficial because I figured out that two parts of the roots were incorrect and was able to fix them for the final.
